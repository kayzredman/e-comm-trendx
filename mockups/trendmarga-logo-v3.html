Designer notes

  • Why lowercase + capital M? The camelCase wordmark is the rarest setting in fashion/commerce branding (eBay tried it; almost no one else). It signals tech-fluent, modern, and slightly disruptive. The capital M does triple duty: it's the pivot in the wordmark, it's the only short letter in tMg, and it becomes the favicon focal point.
  • Why these fonts (not Inter)? Inter is the system default everywhere — it can never be your logo. Clash Display, Cabinet Grotesk, General Sans, and Switzer are all free for commercial use via Fontshare. They give TrendMarga a real voice while staying cheap to license.
  • One vs two color? Brand primary is two-color (ink + accent on the M). One-color is a fallback — used for stamps, embroidery, print bleeds, tiny UI. Both must look complete on their own.
  • Short form tMg: Used in favicons, app icons, social avatars, watermarks. Never used at large sizes where the full wordmark would fit.
  • Clear space: Equal to the cap-height of "M" on all sides. Never tighter.
  • Don't do: Don't rotate. Don't outline (except embroidery). Don't put the accent on any letter other than "M." Don't switch case.
