Construction rules (apply to all concepts)

  • Wordmark: Inter 800, tracking -0.025em. Two-tone: "Trend" in #0B1220, "Marga" in chosen accent.
  • Mark on grid: 120×120 viewBox, 8pt construction grid, 24px corner radius for app-icon containers.
  • Min size: Mark works down to 16px (decorative details drop out below 32px). Wordmark needs ≥80px width.
  • Clear space: Equal to height of capital "T" in the wordmark on all sides.
  • On color: Knockout to white on primary blue. Use accent-light tint on dark backgrounds (e.g. #C4B5FD on ink for violet).
  • Favicon: Use mark only with solid blue background — no wordmark below 24px.
