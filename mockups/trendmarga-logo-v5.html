Designer notes

  • The split shifted. trendM (including the capital M) now lives in blue. arga drops to ash. The pulse continues to live just past the wordmark as the brand's heartbeat.
  • Why this works. The blue half is the active half — it carries the brand name and the pivot letter (M). The ash half is the supporting tail. This mirrors how brand reads in language: "trendM…" is the punch, "arga" is the resolution.
  • Dark mode shift. Use the lighter tint of the chosen blue (e.g. #60A5FA for cobalt, #3B82F6 for royal) on dark backgrounds so it stays vibrant against ink.
  • Favicon parity. Tile keeps ink black, the t stays white, the M takes the brand blue, and the inline pulse dot is the punctuation. Reads as tM• — the wordmark in miniature.
  • Mono fallback. For one-color print, the whole wordmark renders in ink. The blue half stays at 100% black; the ash half drops to 50% grey.
