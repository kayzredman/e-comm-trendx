Designer notes

  • The custom t. Inline SVG. Flat bottom (no curl), slight foot extension on the right for stability. Inherits currentColor so it picks up whatever the wordmark color is — black, white, anything.
  • Why black + ash, not blue? Drops the previous brand-blue dependency. Ash #9CA3AF for "Marga" half + black for "trend" + capital M = built-in two-tone hierarchy that needs zero color decisions in 90% of UI.
  • The pulse is the brand. Single accent color, used only on the pulse dot, primary CTAs, and live-status indicators. Everything else stays mono. This is why pulse color choice matters more than anything else in this v4.
  • Favicon strategy. Favicon = tM — the custom flat-bottom t paired with the bold capital M, on a rounded ink tile. Preserves the camelCase signature, carries both hero glyphs from the wordmark, and reads instantly as trendMarga.
  • Favicon sizing. Pulse dot is omitted at 16px (the tile is already crowded by the two letters). At 24px+ the dot returns to the top-right at ~12% of the tile size.
  • Animation. Pulse breathes 1× every 2.2s in product UI. In static print/PDFs the dot is just solid.
