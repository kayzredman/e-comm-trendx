Construction notes (same across all variants)
  • Four chevrons meeting at a single 4px ink-dot center — reads as both an X and a compass/crossroad (commerce + trend).
  • Two diagonal pairs use opposing gradients so the X has visual depth at large sizes and resolves to two flat colors at 16px.
  • Wordmark: Inter 800, tight tracking (-0.025em). Final letter inherits the accent color so the lockup feels intentional, not decorative.
  • Center dot drops out at <32px to keep the silhouette crisp on favicons.
  • App icon padding: 24px radius rounded square = iOS/Android-safe.
